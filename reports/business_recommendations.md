# Business Recommendations

**Company:** Nordstern Commerce  
**Analysis window:** 2024-01-01 to 2025-12-31  
**Snapshot date:** 2026-01-01  

Recommendations are decision hypotheses derived from the synthetic portfolio dataset. Each should be validated with a controlled test before broad rollout.

## 1. Increase controlled acquisition tests in Affiliate

- **Priority:** High
- **Evidence:** Matured customers acquired through Affiliate generated EUR 67.06 per acquired customer in 90 days, 123.4% above the all-channel average.
- **Recommended action:** Reallocate 10% of marginal prospecting budget for a four-week incrementality test; guard on CAC payback and volume quality.
- **Owner:** Growth Marketing
- **Expected impact:** Higher 90-day revenue per acquired customer

## 2. Reduce checkout abandonment for Social

- **Priority:** High
- **Evidence:** Social has the weakest checkout-to-order rate at 70.0%. A 2 percentage-point lift is worth about 26 orders / EUR 3,655 at current AOV.
- **Recommended action:** Test guest checkout, payment-message clarity, and mobile form simplification; measure incremental completed orders.
- **Owner:** Product & CRO
- **Expected impact:** Approximately EUR 3,655 revenue per observed period

## 3. Launch a value-based win-back program for at-risk customers

- **Priority:** High
- **Evidence:** 982 at-risk customers represent EUR 282,189 in historical net revenue.
- **Recommended action:** Prioritize high-monetary customers, personalize by last purchased category, and hold out 10% to measure incrementality.
- **Owner:** CRM & Retention
- **Expected impact:** Improved 90-day retention without blanket discounting

## 4. Address returns in Fashion

- **Priority:** Medium
- **Evidence:** Fashion has the highest average product return rate at 8.7%, putting revenue and service costs at risk.
- **Recommended action:** Audit the top-returned SKUs, enrich product-detail content, and test fit/compatibility guidance before scaling promotions.
- **Owner:** Merchandising
- **Expected impact:** Lower refunds and better contribution margin

## 5. Protect momentum in the South region

- **Priority:** Medium
- **Evidence:** Net revenue grew 82.1% in the latest quarter versus the prior quarter, the strongest regional trend.
- **Recommended action:** Validate whether growth is repeatable by channel and category, then localize inventory and campaigns around the winning mix.
- **Owner:** Commercial Analytics
- **Expected impact:** Sustained regional growth with inventory support
